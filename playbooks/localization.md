# Localization and format adaptation

## Purpose

Reuse approved source media across languages and ratios while checking each resulting experience.

## Input contract

Approved source media, editable text and audio, target locales such as EN/ES/PT, target ratios, glossary, rights, and local reviewers.

## Steps

1. Inventory reusable source media and separate text, captions, voice, and graphic layers.
2. Adapt meaning, CTA, terminology, and pronunciation for each locale; identify regional variants explicitly.
3. Create each ratio from the approved source, adjusting crops, text wrapping, subtitles, and safe areas.
4. Review every locale/ratio combination for comprehension, timing, visual integrity, and required disclosures.

## Acceptance evidence

Locale/ratio matrix, source master hash, translation review, and final files with per-variant checks.

## Fail and repair rules

Do not mark all variants complete from one successful render. Repair truncated text, poor crops, mistranslations, or timing locally and rerun affected checks.

## Synthetic example

A synthetic EN source becomes ES and PT versions in 9:16 and 1:1. Six outputs reuse licensed footage, while text and narration are separately reviewed.

## Limitations

The kit does not translate, dub, reframe, or verify cultural suitability. Reuse rights and language quality require external review.
