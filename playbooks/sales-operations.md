# Sales operations

## Purpose

Prepare grounded sales material while keeping research and outreach permissions distinct.

## Input contract

Offer, audience, source dates, pricing sources, permitted claims, deliverable scope, and explicit outreach authorization if applicable.

## Steps

1. Check pricing and product details against current authoritative sources; record access dates and uncertainty.
2. Separate substantiated claims from assumptions. Omit unsupported ROI promises and invented customer outcomes.
3. Prepare the lead research, qualification notes, and draft messages within the authorized scope.
4. Before any external contact, verify explicit outreach authorization, recipients, channel, and final content with the operator.

## Acceptance evidence

Dated source list, claim substantiation, research deliverable, and separate outreach authorization when sending is in scope.

## Fail and repair rules

Stale pricing requires re-verification. Unsupported ROI claims must be removed or clearly scoped as hypothetical calculations. Permission to prepare leads never implies permission to contact them.

## Synthetic example

A synthetic brief requests a prospect shortlist. The operator delivers researched accounts and draft copy, removes an unsubstantiated savings claim, and sends nothing without outreach authorization.

## Limitations

The kit neither researches live sources nor sends outreach. It cannot determine legal compliance, commercial accuracy, or recipient consent.
