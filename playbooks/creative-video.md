# Creative video production

## Purpose

Produce a coherent video with distinct scenes, stable styling, and verified sound and motion.

## Input contract

Approved brief, audience, script, scene purposes, style references, brand assets, duration, and delivery ratios.

## Steps

1. Create a scene plan with a distinct visual purpose for each beat and a fixed style sheet.
2. Generate a short representative draft; compare identity, palette, typography, framing, and motion against the brief.
3. Inspect the complete render for repeated imagery, style drift, speech alignment, safe text placement, and ending CTA.
4. Route the final render through independent QA before preparing a publishing handoff.

## Acceptance evidence

Scene map, style reference, final render hash, timed review notes, and required quality checks.

## Fail and repair rules

Repeated scenes or drift require targeted regeneration. Critical audio or lip-sync defects block delivery; attractive frames do not offset them.

## Synthetic example

A synthetic product explainer repeats the same desk shot in four beats. Replace two scenes with use and outcome shots, then review the full sequence again.

## Limitations

The kit does not render, generate media, license assets, or score aesthetics. Operators must define the quality rubric.
